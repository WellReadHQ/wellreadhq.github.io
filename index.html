How it works

  1. 1

    Connect

    The Edge agent reads OPC UA, MQTT or PI read-only and passes readings to WellRead on your server.

  2. 2

    Learn normal

    WellRead learns what normal looks like for each instrument from its own history.

  3. 3

    Warn early

    Operators get grouped, prioritised alerts while there is still time to act.

Built for the control room

3D and P&ID views

A live 3D plant overview and a 2D P&ID symbol view, on phone and desktop. Only equipment with a problem stands out.

Alarms & alerts

One shared screen for alarms and alerts, with acknowledge and close by role, and a thin alarm strip that is always in view.

Operating states

Running, Startup, Shutdown, Maintenance per site or unit, so planned work does not flood operators with noise.

Approvals & audit

State changes are requested with a reason and approved by someone else. Every change is in the audit trail.

Reading quality

Each reading is good, uncertain or bad. Only good readings are evaluated; stuck, standing and chattering sensors are flagged grey.

Runs on your server

Installed inside your network on Linux or Windows. Your plant data and staff accounts stay with you.
